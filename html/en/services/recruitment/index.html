01 | Recruitment Services

Connecting the potential of people and businesses.

MIRAI VIET NAM provides candidates with the expertise, language skills, and experience to meet the recruitment needs of Japanese companies, as well as foreign-invested companies in Vietnam and Vietnamese companies.

We look beyond a company's hiring requirements to comprehensively assess each candidate's experience, career direction, and growth potential, aiming for a strong fit and long-term commitment.

Key Services

Recruitment of Japanese-speaking talent

Technical talent in IT, mechanical engineering, construction, and other fields

Logistics, marketing, import-export, and administrative/management talent

Mid-level and senior management, and specialist talent

Placement of Japanese talent to work in Vietnam

Placement of foreign talent residing in Japan

Specified Skilled Worker talent (Tokutei Ginō)

Support throughout the recruitment process and procedures until the candidate starts work
