03 | Business Connect & Support

Turning Vietnam-Japan connections into real business opportunities.

MIRAI VIET NAM provides two-way support: helping Japanese companies grow in the Vietnamese market and helping Vietnamese companies access the Japanese market.

We go beyond simply introducing companies or products, providing end-to-end support from identifying potential customers, distributors, and business partners to arranging meetings, negotiations, and post-meeting follow-up.

Japan → Vietnam

Supporting business and sales activities in the Vietnamese market.

  • Identifying potential customers
  • Finding agents and distributors
  • Arranging and supporting business meetings
  • Market and competitor research
  • Support with negotiations and deal follow-up with Vietnamese companies

Vietnam → Japan

Supporting the development of sales channels in Japan.

  • Identifying companies and buyers in Japan
  • Connecting with importers and distributors in Japan
  • Support in preparing company and product introduction materials in Japanese
  • Support with discussions and negotiations
  • Support for Vietnamese companies entering the Japanese market
