About MIRAI VIET NAM

MIRAI VIET NAM is a business partner connecting Vietnam and Japan, focusing on three core areas: recruitment services, business consulting, business connections and market development support.

Mission Values Message Company Profile MIRAI JAPAN & Offices

Mission

Connecting the potential of Vietnam and Japan, creating a new future.


Japan is home to many businesses with outstanding technology, products, services and management expertise. Vietnam, meanwhile, has a young workforce, a growing market, and a business ecosystem with increasingly competitive production capabilities.


MIRAI VIET NAM connects the "People – Businesses – Markets" of both countries, turning each side's strengths into new business opportunities.


Our mission goes beyond simple introductions or intermediary services - we work alongside our clients to find solutions, develop markets together, and become a long-term partner throughout their growth journey.

MIRAI VIET NAM's Values

People × Business × Market


MIRAI VIET NAM's strength is not limited to recruitment. We leverage our talent network and business network to address the business needs of companies in both Vietnam and Japan.

Need to find the right talent.

Want to enter the Vietnamese market.

Want to develop new customers.

Want to bring products into the Japanese market.

Want to find a trustworthy business partner.

MIRAI VIET NAM stands by clients on each specific challenge, working toward turning business opportunities into reality.

Message

Bringing Vietnam and Japan closer together.


The economic relationship between Vietnam and Japan continues to expand across many fields, including manufacturing, human resources, trade, services and investment.


However, differences in language, business practices, market information and relationship networks mean that many capable businesses and individuals have yet to fully seize the cooperation opportunities between the two countries.


MIRAI VIET NAM aims to be the bridge that closes that gap — connecting people with businesses, connecting businesses with markets, and creating new business opportunities together between Vietnam and Japan.
