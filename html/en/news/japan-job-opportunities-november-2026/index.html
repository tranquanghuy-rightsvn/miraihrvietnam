JAPAN JOB OPPORTUNITIES – NOVEMBER 2026

Explore Career Opportunities in Japan – Build Your Future with Japanese Companies!

MIRAI JAPAN is pleased to introduce a selection of job opportunities available in Japan for November 2026. This month's recruitment bulletin features positions across various industries, including mechanical engineering, food service, and child development support.

Whether you are looking to develop your professional skills, gain international work experience, or build a long-term career in Japan, MIRAI is here to support your journey.

Featured Job Opportunities – November 2026

1. POSITION 1: CNC/NC Mechanical Engineer

Number of vacancies: 7 male candidates

Work locations:

  • Shizuoka: 3 positions

  • Nagano: 2 positions

  • Toyama: 2 positions

Job Responsibilities

  • Operate CNC/NC machines to manufacture plastic components.

  • Perform mechanical machining operations in a clean and comfortable manufacturing environment.

Salary & Benefits

  • Basic wage: JPY 1,250/hour

  • Housing allowance: JPY 10,000/month

  • Average overtime: Approximately 60 hours/month

Requirements

  • Male candidates.

  • Graduates majoring in Mechanical Manufacturing or related fields, or candidates whose academic transcripts include relevant mechanical manufacturing subjects.

  • Candidates with limited Japanese language proficiency are also welcome to apply.

  • Applications will be reviewed based on academic certificates and transcripts.

Working Environment

  • Clean workplace without unpleasant odors.

  • Friendly colleagues and a comfortable working environment.

  • Relatively manageable job responsibilities.

2. POSITION 2: Kitchen Staff – Food Service Industry (Specified Skilled Worker Type 1)

Number of vacancies: 8

Work locations: Okinawa, Japan

  • Onna Village, Kunigami District (Maeganeku, Fuchaku and Nakama)

  • Motobu Town, Kunigami District

Job Responsibilities

  • Prepare ingredients and cook meals according to company recipes.

  • Manage kitchen inventory.

  • Maintain kitchen hygiene and food safety standards.

Salary & Benefits

  • Basic monthly salary: JPY 218,000

  • Estimated monthly income: Approximately JPY 250,000, including overtime.

  • Working hours: 14:00–23:00 (8 working hours plus break time).

  • Two days off per week on a shift basis.

  • Full social insurance coverage.

  • Staff meals provided.

  • Company dormitory available from JPY 25,000/month, including electricity, water and gas.

Requirements

  • Applicants must currently reside in Japan.

  • Hold a valid Specified Skilled Worker (SSW) Type 1 status in the Food Service Industry and wish to change employers; OR

  • Hold a qualifying residence status such as Permanent Resident, Long-Term Resident, or Spouse of a Japanese National.

  • Japanese language proficiency: N4 or above (N3 preferred).

  • Age: 18 or above (guideline).

Career Development

  • Support for transitioning to Specified Skilled Worker Type 2.

  • Opportunities for long-term employment and career development in Japan.

3. POSITION 3: Child Development Support Staff 

Number of vacancies: Based on the employer's recruitment plan

Work locations: Various locations across Japan, depending on assignment.

Job Responsibilities

  • Communicate and interact with children.

  • Provide individualized developmental support and educational activities according to each child's needs.

  • Transport children using company vehicles.

  • Communicate with parents and guardians during pick-up and drop-off.

Salary & Benefits

  • During the six-month probationary period: From JPY 1,074/hour.

  • After completing the six-month probationary period: From JPY 211,900/month.

  • Annual bonus and performance-based year-end bonus, subject to company performance.

  • Salary increases based on work experience.

  • Transportation expenses covered according to company regulations.

  • Full social insurance coverage.

  • Company-paid health examinations and influenza vaccinations.

  • Retirement benefit scheme after three years of employment.

  • Company accommodation available from JPY 35,000/month (utilities and internet charged separately).

Working Hours

  • Shift-based schedule between 08:00 and 19:30.

  • 8 working hours per day, with a 60-minute break.

  • Actual working schedules may vary depending on the assigned facility.

Days Off & Leave

  • Two days off per week (Sundays fixed).

  • 108 annual days off.

  • 10 days of paid annual leave in the first year.

  • New Year holidays (December 31 – January 3).

  • Maternity, childcare and nursing care leave.

  • Birthday leave and healthcare leave.

Application Requirements

Applicants must pass the Specified Skilled Worker (SSW) Nursing Care Type 1 examination before applying for the relevant residence status. In addition, applicants must meet at least one of the following educational or professional requirements:

  • Bachelor's degree in Social Welfare.

  • Bachelor's degree in Psychology.

  • Bachelor's degree in Education.

  • Graduate degree in Social Welfare, Psychology or Education.

  • Certified Social Worker qualification.

  • Certified Mental Health Social Worker qualification.

No previous work experience is required. On-the-job training (OJT) and other training support will be provided.
