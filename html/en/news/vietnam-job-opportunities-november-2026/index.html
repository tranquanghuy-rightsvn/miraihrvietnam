VIETNAM JOB OPPORTUNITIES – NOVEMBER 2026

In November 2026, Mirai Viet Nam HR Consulting continues to partner with Japanese, European, American and other international companies in Vietnam to recruit high-quality professionals and management talent.

This month, Mirai is introducing five exciting career opportunities in Ho Chi Minh City, covering various industries, including manufacturing, information technology, textiles and apparel, finance and accounting, and international business development.

These opportunities are open to Vietnamese professionals with relevant expertise and foreign language skills, as well as Japanese candidates seeking career opportunities in Vietnam.

I. CURRENT JOB OPENINGS

1. JAPANESE BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT MANAGER

Industry: Rigid Plastic Packaging / Injection Molding

Location: Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam

Salary: USD 4,000–5,000/month. A higher package may be negotiable for qualified candidates currently based in Vietnam.

Employer: A leading Austrian multinational company specializing in innovative plastic packaging solutions, including bottles, caps and injection-moulded products for international brands.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Lead business development and marketing activities in the Japanese market.

  • Acquire new customers, expand market presence and develop potential business segments.

  • Establish and maintain long-term relationships with Japanese customers and multinational companies.

  • Conduct market research and analyze competitors and industry trends.

  • Negotiate pricing, contracts and commercial terms while managing business project progress.

  • Coordinate with technical, production, quality and operations teams to meet customer requirements.

Requirements:

  • Japanese candidates are preferred, with the ability to work in Vietnam or other countries in the region.

  • Relevant business development, sales or marketing experience in the rigid plastic packaging and injection molding industry, particularly in the Japanese market.

  • Strong analytical, negotiation and customer development skills.

  • Good English communication skills.

  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office.

Benefits:

  • Housing allowance.

  • Support with work permit procedures and related employment documentation.

  • International working environment and career development opportunities within a multinational corporation.

2. TECHNICAL PRE-SALES ENGINEER / TECHNICAL PRE-SALES CONSULTANT

Industry: Information Technology / Digital Transformation

Location: Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam

Salary: USD 2,500–3,000/month

Employer: European technology company.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Work directly with the CEO and provide technical consulting and pre-sales support to clients.

  • Analyze client needs, business challenges and technical requirements.

  • Translate business requirements into practical and feasible technology solutions.

  • Collaborate with Solution Architects, Technical Managers and Delivery Heads to develop solution proposals.

  • Prepare and deliver technical proposals, solution documentation and presentations.

  • Support the sales team in client consulting and commercial negotiations.

  • Stay updated on technology trends, digital transformation solutions and competitor activities.

Requirements:

  • Bachelor's degree in Information Technology, Computer Science or a related field.

  • At least 5 years of experience in Technical Consulting, IT Project Management or Technical Pre-Sales.

  • Good understanding of digital solutions and solution architecture.

  • Japanese proficiency at JLPT N2 level or above, with the ability to communicate professionally in Japanese.

  • Good English communication and writing skills.

  • Strong presentation, analytical and client consulting skills.

Benefits:

  • International working environment with direct reporting to the CEO.

  • Opportunities to participate in technology and digital transformation projects.

  • Continuous professional development and training.

  • Performance-based bonuses, private health insurance and other employee benefits.

3. PROJECT COORDINATOR (JAPANESE – ENGLISH – VIETNAMESE)

Industry: Fashion / Apparel / Textiles / Import & Export

Location: Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam

Salary: USD 1,000–1,200 Gross/month

Employer: An American company operating in the fashion and textile products industry.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Coordinate communication and business activities between team members and business partners in Japan and Vietnam.

  • Provide interpretation and translation in Japanese, English and Vietnamese when required.

  • Prepare reports, meeting minutes, presentations and project-related documents.

  • Coordinate meeting schedules, monitor project progress and follow up on assigned tasks.

  • Conduct market research and collect information on potential partners in food exports, fashion and textile exports.

  • Support new business development projects and market expansion activities.

Requirements:

  • Good Japanese and English communication skills.

  • Ability to communicate professionally with Japanese business partners.

  • Good computer skills and document management capabilities.

  • Proactive attitude, strong sense of responsibility and teamwork skills.

  • Candidates with study or working experience in Japan are preferred.

  • Fresh graduates and entry-level candidates are welcome to apply.

Benefits:

  • Opportunity to work in an international business environment.

  • Opportunity to use Japanese, English and Vietnamese professionally.

  • Training in project management and business development.

  • Career development opportunities in Project Management, Business Development and Sales Planning.

4. GARMENT SALES MANAGER – JAPAN MARKET

Industry: Textile / Apparel / Garment Manufacturing & Trading

Location: Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam

Salary: USD 5,000–6,000/month

Target Candidates: Japanese nationals who are willing to relocate to and work in Vietnam on a long-term basis.

Employer: One of Vietnam's leading companies in textile and apparel manufacturing and trading, with extensive international business operations.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Lead sales and business development activities in the Japanese market.

  • Develop new customers and maintain strategic relationships with major clients, including New Balance Japan and other Japanese fashion and sportswear companies.

  • Promote integrated manufacturing solutions covering yarn, fabric and finished garments.

  • Coordinate with R&D, production, QA and other departments to develop products and meet customer requirements.

  • Manage the entire sales process, including costing, quotations, commercial negotiations and order execution.

  • Analyze Japanese market trends and develop customer acquisition strategies.

  • Manage sales performance, KPIs and project progress.

Requirements:

  • Japanese nationality with native-level Japanese proficiency.

  • Relevant experience in the textile, apparel or garment manufacturing industry.

  • Experience in Sales, Business Development, Product Development or Key Account Management.

  • Good understanding of the textile and apparel value chain, Japanese business practices, costing and quality standards.

  • Good working-level English communication skills.

  • Willingness to live and work in Vietnam.

Benefits:

  • Competitive salary of USD 5,000–6,000/month.

  • Guaranteed 13th-month salary, paid before Lunar New Year.

  • Annual KPI bonus paid in March.

  • Company-paid PVI private health insurance.

  • Transportation arrangements between accommodation and the workplace.

  • One round-trip airline ticket per year.

  • 12 days of annual leave.

  • Annual salary review every April.

5. CHIEF ACCOUNTANT

Industry: Garment Manufacturing and Trading

Location: Tan Binh District, Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam

Salary: Negotiable

Key Responsibilities:

  • Lead and oversee the company's overall accounting and finance operations.

  • Manage accounting records and review monthly, quarterly and annual financial statements.

  • Ensure compliance with Vietnamese accounting standards, tax regulations, corporate laws and financial disclosure requirements applicable to listed companies.

  • Oversee tax declarations, tax finalization and coordination with tax authorities and external auditors.

  • Monitor operating costs, cash flow, accounts receivable, accounts payable, inventory and fixed assets.

  • Develop and improve internal financial controls, accounting procedures and financial risk management.

  • Lead budgeting, forecasting and financial performance analysis.

  • Manage, train and develop the accounting team.

Requirements:

  • Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Auditing, Finance or a related discipline.

  • A valid Chief Accountant Certificate is mandatory.

  • 8–10 years of experience in Accounting & Finance, including at least 3 years as a Chief Accountant.

  • Previous experience working for a listed company is mandatory.

  • Manufacturing industry experience is preferred.

  • Strong knowledge of Vietnamese Accounting Standards (VAS), tax regulations and corporate law.

  • Practical experience with ERP/accounting systems.

  • Good English communication skills.

Benefits:

  • Guaranteed 13th-month salary, paid before Lunar New Year.

  • Annual KPI bonus paid in March.

  • Lunch allowance.

  • Transportation allowance of VND 10,000 per working day.

  • Annual salary review every April.

  • PVI private health insurance for employees at Manager level and above.
