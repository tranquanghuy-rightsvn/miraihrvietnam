MIRAI IS HIRING: GARMENT SALES MANAGER – JAPAN MARKET

MIRAI IS HIRING: GARMENT SALES MANAGER – JAPAN MARKET

Location: Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam
Salary: VND 50,000,000 – 80,000,000/month
Industry: Textile & Garment – Fashion – Sportswear
Position Level: Senior Management

1. ABOUT THE OPPORTUNITY

MIRAI is currently recruiting a Garment Sales Manager – Japan Market for a company operating in the textile and garment industry.

This is an excellent opportunity for an experienced business development professional with a strong background in the textile and garment sector, a solid understanding of the Japanese market, and the ability to build strategic relationships with international fashion and sportswear brands.

The successful candidate will lead business development activities with New Balance Japan and other key Japanese customers, leveraging the company's integrated manufacturing capabilities across yarn, fabric, and finished garments.

2. KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

Business Development – Japan Market

  • Lead business development activities with New Balance Japan and key customers in the fashion and sportswear industries.

  • Build and maintain direct relationships with purchasing, material sourcing, product development, and quality management teams at Japanese customer headquarters.

  • Identify opportunities for cost structure analysis, product development, and supplier registration.

  • Present the company's integrated manufacturing capabilities, covering yarn, fabric, and finished garments.

  • Propose knitted fabrics, functional materials, recycled materials, and suitable product solutions.

Sales Management & Commercial Development

  • Conduct market research, identify target customers, and develop customer acquisition strategies.

  • Manage the entire sales cycle, from product development inquiries and sampling to costing, quotation, negotiation, order conversion, and seasonal follow-up.

  • Monitor Japanese market trends and competitor activities.

  • Manage the sales pipeline, commercial performance, profit margins, and KPI reporting to the Sales Director.

Cross-functional Coordination & Customer Relationship Management

  • Work closely with R&D, Yarn, Fabric Sales, Garment, QA, and Sustainability teams.

  • Ensure customer requirements regarding quality, delivery, traceability, and communication are met.

  • Support customer factory visits, factory assessments, and trade exhibitions.

  • Travel regularly to Japan to maintain customer relationships, resolve issues, and develop long-term business partnerships.

3. REQUIREMENTS

  • Bachelor's degree or above, preferably in Textile Engineering, Garment, Business Administration, International Trade, or a related field.

  • Mandatory: Relevant working experience in the textile and garment industry.

  • Strong understanding of the knitted textile and garment value chain, Japanese customer business processes, costing, quality standards, and product development.

  • Fluent Japanese for business communication. JLPT N1 certification and previous study or working experience in Japan are preferred.

  • Good working proficiency in English. Korean language skills are an advantage.

  • Strong negotiation, presentation, solution-selling, and customer relationship management skills.

  • Strong commercial and analytical mindset, with the ability to manage projects, sales pipelines, profit margins, and business performance reports.

  • Proficient in Microsoft Office.

  • Willingness to travel to Japan regularly.

  • Proactive, responsible, customer-oriented, detail-conscious, and culturally sensitive.

4. COMPENSATION & BENEFITS

  • Monthly Salary: VND 50,000,000 – 80,000,000.

  • Fixed 13th-month salary, paid before Lunar New Year.

  • Annual KPI bonus, paid in March.

  • Lunch allowance.

  • Transportation allowance.

  • Annual salary review in April.

  • PVI private health insurance for management positions at Manager level and above.

5. HOW TO APPLY

If you have a strong background in garment business development, extensive knowledge of the Japanese market, and the ability to manage strategic customer relationships, we would like to hear from you.

Please submit your updated CV to MIRAI, highlighting:

  • Business development experience in the Japanese market.

  • Experience in the textile and garment industry, particularly knitting and garment manufacturing.

  • Experience working with Japanese customers or international brands.

  • Japanese and English language proficiency.
