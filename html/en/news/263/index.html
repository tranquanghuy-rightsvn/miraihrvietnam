Part 7 | Specified Skilled Worker (Tokutei Ginō): A New Gateway for Vietnamese Talent to Japan

In Part 6 — What Do Japanese Companies Need to Prepare When Entering the Vietnamese Market, MIRAI VIET NAM shared the key points Japanese companies should keep in mind when expanding their operations in Vietnam. In this Part 7, we look at the reverse perspective: job opportunities in Japan for Vietnamese talent through the "Specified Skilled Worker" (Tokutei Ginō) residence status — one of the pathways that has been drawing growing interest from both candidates and companies in recent years.

What Is Specified Skilled Worker (Tokutei Ginō)?

Tokutei Ginō is a residence status for foreign workers with professional skills that match real hiring needs in Japan, applicable across a wide range of industries. Compared to some other common labor visa categories, Tokutei Ginō is aimed at candidates who already have a certain level of skill or experience and are hired directly by Japanese companies for specific positions.

Why Are Japanese Companies Interested in Specified Skilled Workers?

Many Japanese companies, especially in manufacturing, construction, and services, are looking for a stable and skilled foreign workforce. Hiring Specified Skilled Workers is one solution that helps companies supplement their workforce with qualified professionals, while also giving foreign candidates the opportunity to gain hands-on work experience in Japan.
