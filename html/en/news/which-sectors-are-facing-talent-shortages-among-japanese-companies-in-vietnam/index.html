Which Sectors Are Facing Talent Shortages Among Japanese Companies in Vietnam?

The demand for highly skilled professionals is becoming a major recruitment challenge for Japanese companies operating in Vietnam, particularly for positions requiring both technical expertise and Japanese language proficiency.

According to JETRO's FY2025 survey, 48.2% of Japanese companies in Vietnam reported that recruitment had become more difficult over the previous two years.

5 Key Sectors Facing Recruitment Challenges

1. Information Technology (IT)

Demand is focused on Software Engineers, Bridge System Engineers (BrSE), IT Communicators, Project Managers, and Digital Transformation Specialists, particularly candidates with both technical expertise and Japanese language skills.

2. Engineering and Manufacturing

Key positions include Mechanical Engineers, Automation Engineers, QA/QC Specialists, and Production Managers. According to JETRO's 2023 survey, the labor shortage rate at factories in Vietnam was reported at 49%.

3. Logistics and Supply Chain Management (SCM)

Companies require experienced professionals in warehouse management, procurement, import-export operations, cost optimization, and international supply chain coordination.

4. Sales and Business Development

Positions such as Business Development Managers, Sales Managers, and Japanese-speaking B2B Sales Professionals are important for customer acquisition and market expansion.

5. Middle and Senior Management

Positions such as General Managers, Factory Managers, HR Managers, and Finance Managers require a combination of leadership capabilities, practical experience, and the ability to collaborate effectively with Japanese management.

These five sectors represent notable recruitment demand areas and do not constitute an official ranking of talent shortages.
