TECHNICAL PRE-SALES ENGINEER（IT）

About the Position

Mirai Viet Nam HR Consulting is supporting a French company specializing in Information Technology and Digital Transformation in recruiting an experienced Technical Pre-Sales Engineer based in Ho Chi Minh City.

Reporting directly to the CEO, this position provides end-to-end pre-sales support by combining strong technical knowledge of digital solutions with a deep understanding of client business requirements. The successful candidate will design practical solutions that address customer needs while contributing to business growth.

Key Responsibilities

  • Engage with clients to identify and analyze their business challenges, needs and technical requirements.
  • Translate complex business requirements into practical, feasible and high-quality technical solutions.
  • Collaborate closely with the Delivery Head, Technical Manager and Solution Architect to develop high-value solutions tailored to each client.
  • Prepare proposals, solution documentation and presentations.
  • Present proposed solutions clearly and persuasively to customers.
  • Provide technical consultation and respond to client inquiries throughout the pre-sales process.
  • Work with the Partnership team to provide technical insights and support customer negotiations.
  • Collect, analyze and document business and technical requirements.
  • Stay up to date with emerging technologies, industry trends and competitor activities to identify new business opportunities.

Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology or a related technical discipline.
  • Minimum 5 years of experience in Technical Consulting, IT Project Management or a Pre-Sales Engineer role.
  • Strong knowledge of digital solutions and the ability to support solution architecture design.
  • Strong decision-making, problem-solving and critical-thinking skills.
  • Strong written English and effective client communication skills.
  • Excellent presentation skills with a customer-oriented mindset.
  • Leadership potential, initiative and confidence in providing constructive input.
  • Japanese written and verbal communication skills are an advantage.

Benefits & Working Environment

The company offers an empowering and accountable working environment with opportunities to develop through emerging technologies, challenging projects, training and on-the-job coaching. Benefits include annual performance reviews and bonuses, a clear long-term career path, private health insurance, periodic health checks, allowances and other employee support programs, as well as company trips, sports clubs and team-building activities.
