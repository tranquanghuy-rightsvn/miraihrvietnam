Recruitment Trends for Japanese Professionals in Vietnam’s FDI Sector

The expansion of foreign-invested enterprises is creating new career opportunities for Japanese professionals in Vietnam.

Vietnam continues to attract foreign investors thanks to its investment environment, young workforce, strategic position in Southeast Asia, and growing potential in manufacturing, trading, technology, logistics and services.

As foreign-invested enterprises expand their operations, recruitment demand for Japanese professionals in Vietnam is becoming increasingly diversified. Opportunities are no longer limited to Japanese-owned companies. Multinational corporations from Europe, the United States, South Korea, Taiwan and other countries are also seeking Japanese talent to develop markets, manage clients, strengthen business partnerships and support international operations.

Vietnam – An Emerging Market for Japanese Professionals

According to JETRO's 2025 survey, 56.9% of Japanese companies in Vietnam plan to expand their business within the next one to two years, highlighting Vietnam's continued growth potential and attractiveness as an investment destination.

Along with business expansion, companies are facing challenges in recruiting qualified management, professional and internationally experienced talent.

In this environment, Japanese professionals with experience in Vietnam, management capabilities and an understanding of multinational business operations are becoming valuable assets to foreign-invested companies.

Why Are FDI Companies Hiring Japanese Professionals?

Recruitment of Japanese professionals is driven not only by language requirements but also by business strategies and market development needs.

1. Expanding the Japanese Market

FDI companies engaged in international trade, manufacturing, exports or services to Japanese customers need professionals who understand the Japanese market, business culture and partner expectations.

2. Managing Japanese Clients and Business Partners

Japanese professionals can play an important role in maintaining client relationships, negotiating business deals, handling customer requirements and identifying new business opportunities.

3. Managing International Operations

In multinational companies, Japanese professionals can act as a bridge between headquarters, clients, suppliers and local teams in Vietnam, improving communication, project coordination and operational efficiency.

4. Knowledge Transfer and Operational Improvement

In manufacturing, engineering, IT and logistics, experienced Japanese professionals can contribute to technology transfer, quality management, process optimization and operational improvement.

What Makes Japanese Candidates Competitive?

FDI companies increasingly value candidates who combine professional expertise with the ability to adapt to international business environments.

  • Previous work or living experience in Vietnam.

  • English or Vietnamese communication skills.

  • Team management and project management experience.

  • B2B sales, business development or account management experience.

  • Professional expertise in manufacturing, IT, trading, logistics or engineering.

  • The ability to work with multicultural teams and adapt to different business environments.

Japanese professionals with experience in Vietnam and a strong understanding of the local business environment may have a competitive advantage in management, business development and international coordination roles.
