How Serious Is the IT Talent Shortage Among Japanese Companies?

As digital transformation (DX), AI, cloud computing, and automation continue to accelerate, Japan is facing an increasingly serious shortage of IT professionals. This challenge is no longer limited to technology companies; it also affects manufacturers, logistics providers, financial institutions, trading companies, and service businesses.

Japan Could Face a Shortage of Up to 790,000 IT Professionals

According to projections by Japan’s Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry (METI), Japan could face a shortage of approximately 160,000 to 790,000 IT professionals by 2030, depending on the growth of IT demand.

Furthermore, according to the Information-technology Promotion Agency, Japan (IPA), 85.1% of surveyed Japanese companies reported a shortage of human resources needed to drive digital transformation (DX).

These figures indicate that the challenge is not simply a lack of people. Companies are increasingly struggling to find professionals with the right combination of technical expertise and business skills.

What Types of IT Talent Are in Demand?

Demand is particularly strong for professionals with expertise in areas such as:

AI/Data, Cloud, Cybersecurity, Software Development, ERP, and Project Management, as well as professionals who can bridge the gap between business teams and technical teams.

For Japanese companies operating in Vietnam or working with offshore development teams, candidates with the following combination are especially valuable:

IT Expertise + Japanese Language Skills + Experience Working with Japanese Companies

As a result, positions such as IT Manager, Project Manager, BrSE, IT Comtor, Software Engineer, AI/Data Engineer, and ERP Consultant can be difficult to fill through conventional job advertising alone.

Vietnam – A Potential IT Talent Source for Japanese Companies

As Japan’s domestic talent pool becomes increasingly limited, international recruitment is becoming an important option for Japanese companies.

Vietnam has significant potential thanks to its young engineering workforce and a well-established software development and offshore industry serving the Japanese market.

Professionals who combine IT expertise, Japanese language proficiency, and an understanding of Japanese business culture can play a key role in connecting Japanese companies with technical teams in Vietnam.
